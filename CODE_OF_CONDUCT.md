# Code of Conduct

hopper-hq is provided by [FairMind](https://fairmind.ai) and open to everyone who wants to use it or to help. We want taking part to be a good experience for all, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful. Disagree about the work, never about the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give feedback that helps, and accept it the same way.
- Respect other people's time: keep issues and pull requests to the point.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about a person's identity.
- Sexualised language or attention, in any form.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may ask someone to stop, or ban them from the project. To report a problem, write privately to the maintainers: through the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and say it is a conduct report, or use the contact on [fairmind.ai](https://fairmind.ai). Reports are handled in confidence, and we will not retaliate against anyone who makes one in good faith.

This code is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
